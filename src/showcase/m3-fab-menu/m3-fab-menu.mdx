# FAB menu

A FAB that opens into two to six related actions. It replaces the older speed
dial. Use it when "create" has a few flavors, such as a new document, sheet or
folder.

## Try it

Tap the FAB. It shrinks into a round close button in the solid color, and the
actions rise out of it one after another. Tap an action or the close button to
close it.

## The motion

Three things move together, all on the same spring:

1. The FAB goes from an 80px rounded square to a 56px circle.
2. Its icon turns from `add` to `close`.
3. Each action fades up from below, 30ms after the one under it.

The stagger is one custom property per item:

```css
.items li {
  transition-delay: calc(var(--i) * 30ms);
}
```

```html
<li [style.--i]="actions().length - 1 - i">
```

Counting from the bottom makes the nearest action appear first.

## Accessibility

- The FAB has `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and its label changes from "Create" to "Close menu".
- Opening moves focus to the first action. Up and down arrows move between actions.
- Escape closes the menu and puts focus back on the FAB.
- Closed actions are `aria-hidden` and `tabindex="-1"`, so they stay out of the tab order.
